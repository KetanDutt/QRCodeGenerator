    How it works & CSV format

    1. Export a CSV from your HR tool or spreadsheet with one row per employee.
    2. Drop it above. Columns are detected automatically; you can adjust the mapping.
    3. Review the table, data-quality warnings and QR previews.
    4. Download a ZIP with PNG/SVG codes and .vcf files, ready for ID cards.
